# Actero — robots.txt
# RFC 9309: consecutive "User-agent:" lines form ONE group that shares
# the rules below them. A crawler obeys only the most specific group
# naming it — if it has its own group, it ignores every Disallow in the
# "*" group. So every crawler we mean to ALLOW (with the site's normal
# Disallow list applied) must sit inside the SAME group as "*", not in
# a separate "Allow: /" group of its own.
#
# Traditional search engines and AI search/citation crawlers (real-time
# answer generation, not training) are allowed below. GPTBot and
# ClaudeBot are TRAINING crawlers — normally opted out like the block
# further down — but allowed here on purpose. Other training-only
# crawlers stay BLOCKED per EU Directive 2019/790 Article 4 TDM opt-out.
# ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
